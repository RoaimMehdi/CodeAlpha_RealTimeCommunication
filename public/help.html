Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join or invite someone to a meeting?
To join, enter the 6-character room code on the homepage or Join page and click Join Meeting. No account is required for guests!

To invite team members, click the green "Copy link" button in the call header. The link copied will look like http://your-site/r/abc123 which will bring them directly into your room.
Why is my camera or microphone not working?
1. Click the padlock icon in your browser's URL address bar.
2. Make sure Camera and Microphone permissions are set to "Allow".
3. Ensure no other application (Zoom, Teams, Skype) is currently using your webcam.
4. Reload the page to apply new permissions.
How does screen sharing work in CamKnock?
Click the "Share" button in the bottom control bar during any call. Your browser will show a prompt allowing you to pick your entire screen, an application window, or a specific browser tab.

All other participants will instantly see your shared screen in the main spotlight stage. To stop, click the red "Stop" button at the top left of the screen share container or the bottom control bar.
How do I use the collaborative Whiteboard?
Click the "Board" button in the call controls to toggle the whiteboard. Anyone in the meeting can draw simultaneously with real-time sync!
• Select colors from the swatch bar.
• Switch between Pen and Eraser modes.
• Adjust stroke thickness (thin, medium, thick).
• Click "Save PNG" to download a snapshot of the whiteboard to your device.
Do I need an account to use CamKnock?
Guests: No account needed! You can join any room with just a display name and the room code.
Meeting Hosts: Yes, hosts can sign up for a free account to create and manage meeting rooms. Registration requires only your name, email, and password.
What is the file upload size limit?
You can upload and share files up to 25MB per file within the in-meeting chat. Supported formats include images, PDF documents, code snippets, presentations, and archives.
